Imagine a blindfolded person on a hilly landscape trying to reach the lowest point. In ML, your position on the hill = current weights, your height = current error. Goal is to reach the valley = minimum error. Gradient tells you the direction of steepest uphill and how steep it is — so you step in the opposite direction. Learning rate controls step size. Too large = overshoot the valley. Too small = takes forever. Chain rule sends the error signal backwards through all network layers multiplicatively — each layer learns how much it contributed to the final error.
